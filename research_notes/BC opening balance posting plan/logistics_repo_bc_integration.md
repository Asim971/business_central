## Q3. How is "durable posting" implemented (outbox table, background worker, idempotency keys, retries)?

### Takeaway
Durable posting is a Postgres job queue (`integration_jobs`, `JobType.BcWriteCommand = 6`, serialized per `aggregate_key`) plus a command ledger (`bc_write_commands`, unique business key, outcome enum Unobserved/Landed/NotLanded/Undecided/Abandoned). `BcWriteCommandJobHandler` probes BC before every re-attempt and never retries a write in-process; `BcWriteSettlementJobHandler` (JobType 7, constant key `bc-write-settlement`) sweeps unsettled commands and asks BC what it holds. The warehouse post itself is asynchronous in BC: the AL `postRequest` bound action retains an immutable request row (table 70101) and enqueues a native Job Queue Entry running codeunit 70103; the backend records `Undecided` and settles `Landed` only from request-correlated `postedWarehouseShipmentLines`.

### Cited Findings
- `IBcWriteCommand` contract: `string CommandName` (stable, persisted), `string?
